KLIP N STORE
THE MATERIAL

Liquid clarity.
Crystal strength.

Klip N Store is cast from 100% borosilicate glass — melted, shaped and annealed until it holds the clarity of liquid and the resilience of crystal. It won't cloud, stain, or leach, no matter how many years of use you put it through.

EVERYDAY USES

Nine uses. One klip.

01

Store snacks

The four-clip lid locks airtight, so namkeen, nuts and cookies stay crisp for longer.

02

Bake meals in the oven

Borosilicate glass goes straight into the oven. Bake a pasta or a cake, then serve in the same dish.

REMOVE THE LID BEFORE BAKING
03

Store leftovers in the fridge

Clear glass shows what's inside at a glance, and it doesn't hold on to curry stains or smells.

04

Reheat in the microwave

Warm up lunch in the box you stored it in. No transferring, no extra dishes to wash.

REMOVE THE LID BEFORE HEATING
05

Carry meals on the go

Clip it shut and pack it. The seal keeps dal and gravies inside the box, not in your bag.

06

Store baby food

Glass doesn't carry smells from one meal to the next, so purees taste only of what's in them.

07

Marinate meat and fish

Marinate overnight. The seal keeps the juices in and the smell out of the rest of your fridge.

08

Freeze chutneys and purees

Make green chutney or tomato puree in a big batch and freeze it for the weeks ahead.

09

Prep fruits and vegetables

Chop in the morning, stack the boxes in the fridge, and dinner comes together faster.
