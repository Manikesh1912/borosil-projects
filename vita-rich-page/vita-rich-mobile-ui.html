Four thoughtful details that make the Vita Rich Juicer effortless to use, every single day.

Want fresh, nutrient-packed juices every morning? Say hello to the Borosil Slow Juicer! Its low RPM technology extracts every drop of goodness, keeping your juices packed with nutrients (and flavour!). Enjoy smooth and pulp-free drinks, from beetroot juice for your health-conscious mornings to mosambi juicer for those hot afternoons. Plus, it’s so quiet that it won’t even wake up your toddler. Cleaning? Super easy! Healthy mornings are now effortless—and much tastier.

  • 250W Low-Noise Motor: Powerful yet quiet for a peaceful juicing experience.
  • 1.5L Feeder Hopper with Wide Mouth: Large capacity to fit whole fruits for faster, more efficient juicing.
  • 90-Min Non-Stop Running Time: Perfect for large batches, juice your fruits non-stop for up to 90 minutes.
  • Reverse Function: Easily clears out any pulp blockages, ensuring smooth juicing every time.

  • Easy to Use & Clean: Simple assembly and easy-to-clean parts make juicing a breeze.

Do's:

  • Ensure all parts are securely assembled before operating.
  • Cut large fruits or vegetables into smaller pieces for smoother juicing.
  • Use the reverse function if you experience any blockages.
  • Clean the filter and juicing parts after every use to prevent build-up.


    Don’ts:

    • Don’t operate the juicer without the safety switch in place.
    • Avoid using hard pits or seeds, as they may damage the filter.
    • Don’t use abrasive materials to clean the parts—stick to soft brushes or cloths.
    • Don’t overload the hopper; allow space for optimal juicing performance.
